## Entry (126 characters)

> "I can sense exactly when a stranger somewhere on Earth is thinking about me — I just never find out who, or what they thought."

## Why it works

It sounds almost psychic, then collapses: you get the ping but never the who or the what, so it achieves nothing. A stranger somewhere on Earth is concrete and easy to picture in use, the power is unexpected, and the uselessness itself is the joke.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python stdlib only, no network, no clock) gate-checks the line (1–140 characters, single line, no leading/trailing whitespace) and writes it byte-identically to `entry.txt` inside `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (fallback `/output`), with no trailing newline. Deterministic across runs.
